# Rant-to-Launch — Self-Demo Walkthrough

Rant-to-Launch used on its own use case: a real founder rant about the
gap between "having an idea" and "actually shipping a launch", run
through the live app end-to-end. The screenshots below capture every
meaningful step of the flow, in order.

- **Project name used:** Rant-to-Launch
- **Audience:** Solo SaaS founders and indie hackers
- **Tone:** Founder-direct
- **Channels:** X, LinkedIn, Newsletter, Carousel
- **Resulting workspace URL:** `/projects/8d013b1274` (the live
  generated launch package — see `05-workspace-overview.png` →
  `15-final-result.png`)

## Rant used as input

> OK look. I have the idea. I've had the idea for like three weeks. The
> thing actually works on my laptop. I demoed it to two friends and one
> of them literally said "ship this." And I haven't.
>
> Why? Because the second I open a doc to write the launch tweet I freeze.
> Then I think, well I also need a LinkedIn post, those are different.
> And a landing page — which means a hero line, a subhead, three feature
> blocks I haven't named yet, an FAQ, a CTA. Then a newsletter blurb for
> the people who actually subscribed, which is a different voice again.
> Then a carousel for the algorithm gods. Then a storyboard for the demo
> video I keep saying I'll record. Then a posting plan because if I just
> dump it all on Tuesday morning nobody sees it.
>
> So I have like sixteen tabs open with half-written angles and I keep
> rewriting the first sentence of the tweet. The actual product is done.
> The launch is what's killing me. I have a Notion full of voice memos
> and Slack messages to myself at 1am and none of it is in launch shape.
>
> What I want is: I paste this whole mess in one place, I get back a
> clear angle, the tweets, the LinkedIn, the landing copy, the
> newsletter, the carousel, the storyboard, and a 7-day plan that tells
> me what to post when. Then I edit the parts I care about and I ship.
> That's it. That's the whole product I want.
